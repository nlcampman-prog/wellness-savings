## Why 9pm cocoa hits different than 2pm cocoa

Afternoon chocolate is a snack. Night chocolate sits on top of a day you did not eat enough protein, plus a cortisol dip, plus a brain that finally has space to panic about permission slips.

Sugar at that hour is not evil. It is just loud. You fall asleep. Then you bounce.

**What actually worked:**

1. Keep the **mug and the couch**. The ritual is the point.
2. Change the **powder**.
3. If I am actually hungry, add a **real snack** — not a third handful of whatever is in the pantry.

If dinner was a situation, cocoa-only is a blood sugar roller coaster wearing pajamas.

## FAQ

### Is Cheerific caffeine-free?
Read the bag you buy — cocoa has a little theobromine. It is not a latte. If you are extremely caffeine-sensitive, test it on a night you can afford to know.

### Can kids drink it?
Mine have. That is not a serving recommendation for your child. Use your judgment and the label.

### What if I want actual dessert?
Have dessert. This article is for the every-night packet that was pretending to be wellness. Special occasion brownies are not the enemy. The enemy is a daily sugar spike you blame on "hormones" without checking the packet.

### Will this fix insomnia?
No. It might stop making it worse. If you cannot sleep, that is a bigger pile: light, kids, anxiety, iron, the whole circus. I am a former SLP, not a sleep clinic.
