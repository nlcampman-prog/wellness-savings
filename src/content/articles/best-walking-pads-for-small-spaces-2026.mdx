## What to Look For in a Walking Pad

Before the picks, the criteria that separate a good walking pad from a frustrating one:

1. **Noise level:** The whole point is using it while you work or watch TV. A pad that drones above 50 dB will drive you — and your Zoom coworkers — crazy. Look for motors rated under 55 dB.
2. **Foldability:** The best walking pads fold flat or fold in half so they slide under a couch or bed. Check the folded height — under 6 inches is ideal for sofa clearance.
3. **Speed range:** Walking pads top out around 3.7-4 mph — enough for a brisk walk, not a run. If you want jogging, look for a 2-in-1 model that reaches 7+ mph.
4. **Weight capacity and belt width:** Standard is 220-265 lbs capacity and a 15.5-16.5 inch wide belt. If you're taller or bigger, size up.
5. **Stability:** A wobbly pad is worse than no pad. Look for a solid steel frame and check reviews for "shaky" or "wobbly" complaints.
