The first time I "switched to mushroom coffee," I did it on a Monday, cold turkey, because a podcast made it sound like a personality transplant.

By 10am I had a headache that felt like a tax. By 2pm I was in a drive-thru. I told myself mushroom coffee was a lie. It was not a lie. It was withdrawal plus a new flavor plus five children who do not care about Lion's Mane.

I am a mom of five. I like caffeine. I do not like the 11am shake or the 9pm second wind that is really just anxiety in a mug. **Mushroom coffee** is not magic mushrooms and it is not decaf pretending to be a lifestyle. It is usually **less caffeine plus functional mushrooms** (Lion's Mane, cordyceps, sometimes chaga) so the cup is a little kinder. The switch fails when you treat it like sobriety week.

This is the 14-day version that actually stuck. Ranked taste-offs live in [Ryze vs Everyday Dose](/articles/ryze-vs-everyday-dose-2026/) and the [mushroom coffee roundup](/articles/best-mushroom-coffee-alternatives-ryze-everyday-dose-mudwtr-2026/). This is the *process*.

## What you are actually switching

Regular drip in this house is roughly **80–140mg of caffeine** a cup, depending on how aggressive I was with the scoop. Many mushroom coffees land closer to **half of that**, or they keep coffee and add L-theanine / mushrooms so the same caffeine feels less jagged.

You might get:

- a milder lift,
- less of the "I could fight a parking meter" edge,
- a taste that is earthier than Dunkin'.

You will not get a medical cure. If your heart is doing something new, that is a clinician, not a bag of Ryze.

## The 14-day switch (do not skip day 1–3)

**Days 1–3: blend, do not replace.**  
Half regular coffee, half mushroom coffee. Same mug, same morning chaos. Your brain still gets caffeine. Your taste buds start the introduction.

**Days 4–7: first cup is mushroom, second cup is regular if you need it.**  
This is the week people quit because they wanted the second cup to also be a TED Talk. Keep the second cup regular if you have a headache. Headaches are not a character test.

**Days 8–14: two mushroom cups, or one mushroom and tea.**  
If you still want a 2pm espresso, have it. The goal is *less jagged*, not a purity contest.

If you skip the blend week, you will call mushroom coffee disgusting and go back to the gas station roast. I already did that experiment for you.

## Rules that keep you from quitting on Wednesday

- **Do not drop from three coffees to zero mushroom cups.** That is a migraine, not a cleanse.
- **Eat something.** Caffeine on an empty stomach is how "mushroom coffee gave me anxiety" stories start. Often it was hunger.
- **Give the taste ten cups, not two.** Cup two is still "this is not Dunkin'." Cup ten is "this is just my cup."
- **Keep one regular coffee as a tool.** Church potluck coffee can stay terrible. You are not converting the urn.

## FAQ

### Will I get a caffeine headache?
If you jump off a cliff, yes. Blend for three days. That is the whole method.

### Is this decaf?
Usually no. Read the bag. MUD\\WTR is the lower-caffeine vibe. Cuppa is still coffee.

### Can I put it in a regular coffee maker?
Instant goes in a mug. If you buy a ground version, follow that bag. Do not dump instant into a drip basket and email me.

### Is this medical advice?
No. If you are pregnant, have blood pressure issues, or caffeine does something scary, talk to a clinician. I am a former pediatric SLP who drinks coffee next to a high chair.

## The 14-day version, even shorter

Blend half-and-half for three days. First cup mushroom for a week. Then decide. **Cuppa** if you need it to taste like coffee. **Ryze** or **Everyday Dose** if you already know those names. **MUD\\WTR** if you want less caffeine for real. **Javvy** if breakfast is the ritual you will actually keep.

The mushrooms are not the hard part. The hard part is not lying to yourself about how much coffee you were drinking.
