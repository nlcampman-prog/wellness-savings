## Building Your "Mom Gym" — What Actually Matters

After years of accumulating (and shedding) home fitness equipment, here's what I've learned:

1. **One machine you'll actually use beats three you won't.** Start with one. Use it for 30 days. Then add a second if you need variety.
2. **Noise is a dealbreaker.** If a machine rattles, squeaks, or hums loud enough to wake a sleeping child, you simply won't use it. All four machines above pass the nap-time test.
3. **Storage matters more than features.** The best machine is the one you can leave accessible. If it has to be hauled out of a closet and assembled each time, you'll use it half as often.
4. **Content matters.** A basic exercise bike with a good streaming class is vastly more motivating than a fancy machine with no guidance. Budget for a tablet mount and a subscription to whatever fitness content you prefer.
