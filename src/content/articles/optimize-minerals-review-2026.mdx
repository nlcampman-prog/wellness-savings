## The Ionic Difference: Why Form Matters

Here's where most mineral supplements fall short, and I learned this the hard way (after wasting money on a few of them).

A mineral in a cheap supplement is often a **mineral salt** — think rock-dust or oyster-shell calcium. It's not bioavailable. You swallow it, and most of it passes right through you, which is why so many people take magnesium and feel nothing.

**Ionic minerals** are minerals that are already dissolved and carrying an electrical charge — which is the form your cells actually take them up. This is exactly how minerals are delivered to your body in nature: plant roots pull dissolved ionic minerals out of the soil. Optimize Minerals uses concentrated seawater-derived ionic trace minerals, so you're getting the full spectrum — 70+ minerals — not just three isolated ones.

That's why the brand's angle on "bioavailable forms" isn't just marketing. It's the difference between swallowing a rock and actually absorbing the mineral.
