## Why "Clean" Protein Matters for Women's Hormones

For women, protein isn't just about building muscle; it is the building block for our hormones, neurotransmitters, immune cells, and metabolic health. However, the supplement industry is notoriously under-regulated, meaning heavy metals, pesticides, and artificial chemicals frequently end up in popular tubs.

When selecting a truly clean protein shake, here is what you must avoid:
- **Artificial Sweeteners (Sucralose, Aspartame, Acesulfame K):** These are notorious for killing healthy gut bacteria and causing bloating and gas.
- **Thickeners and Gums (Xanthan Gum, Guar Gum, Carrageenan):** Used to make shakes feel "thick," but they irritate the intestinal lining.
- **Heavy Metals:** Always choose brands that perform third-party testing (like NSF Certified for Sport or Clean Label Project) to ensure your protein is free from lead, arsenic, and mercury.
- **Soy Protein Isolates:** Often highly processed and chemically extracted with hexane. Prioritize grass-fed whey, organic plant-based, or grass-fed beef isolate instead.
