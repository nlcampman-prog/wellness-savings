The honest answer is: sometimes both. Usually lunch. Almost never "the powder instead of vegetables and a plate."

## What a greens powder can do

A decent greens drink is **micronutrient insurance**. Vitamins, plant compounds, sometimes probiotics or adaptogens. It is useful when:

- you travel and the only green thing at the hotel is the carpet,
- you are in a season of beige kid food and your own plate is whatever they rejected,
- you are tired of a 12-bottle morning and want one scoop that is *not* trying to be protein.

It cannot:

- replace fiber from actual plants,
- replace protein,
- replace a meal when you are hungry enough to eat a granola bar in the pantry at 10:12am.

If a brand markets itself as "foundational nutrition," believe the *nutrition* part. Do not believe the *foundation of your calorie day* part.

## The decision I actually use

| If this is your morning | Do this |
|---|---|
| You ate eggs / Greek yogurt / leftover protein | Greens optional. Powder is bonus, not breakfast. |
| You had coffee and a vibe | Eat. Then decide if you still want a scoop. |
| You are on a road trip with gas-station options | Greens powder earns its keep. AG1 travel packs or ON3 packets. |
| You are chasing 100g of protein | Do not use AG1 as the strategy. Use [food-first protein](/articles/frugal-protein-strategy/). |
| You hate the taste of every greens you have tried | Organifi before you quit the category. |

**Lunch wins.** A greens powder is what you add when lunch was a granola bar and a handful of your kid's goldfish. It is not a moral upgrade over a salad. It is a backup.

## How I actually take it (so it does not become cupboard archaeology)

- **After food, or with food.** A scoop on an empty stomach is how people "feel nauseous and decide greens don't work."
- **Cold water, drink it. Do not sip it for 40 minutes.** It gets worse as it sits.
- **Five days a week is a habit.** Seven days a week is a personality. I do not have that personality in July.
- **If you miss a day because you ate a pile of vegetables, that is a win.** The powder is the understudy.

## FAQ

### Can a greens powder replace a multivitamin?
Sometimes that is how it is marketed. I still want you to know what is *in* it, and I still want food. If you are pregnant, nursing, or on medication, ask the person who actually manages your labs — I am a former SLP, not your physician.

### Why do I bloat after greens?
Fiber, sugar alcohols, or a probiotic your gut has not met. Cut the serving in half for a week. Switch brands. Do not "push through" a week of misery and call it detox.

### Is AG1 worth it if Organifi is cheaper?
Worth it if you will drink it, you want the testing story, and the price does not make you skip groceries. If the price makes you skip groceries, it is not wellness. It is a subscription.

### What about protein greens?
