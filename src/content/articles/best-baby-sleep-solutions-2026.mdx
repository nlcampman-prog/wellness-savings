## The Womb-to-World Transition: Understanding Baby Sleep

To understand why certain sleep tools work, we must first look at a baby’s biology:

*   **The Fourth Trimester:** For the first three months of life, a newborn is adapting to a vastly different environment. Inside the womb, they were continuously warm, held in a tight space, and surrounded by the low-frequency, loud swooshing sound of their mother's blood flow.
*   **The Moro Reflex:** This is an involuntary startle reflex where a baby suddenly flings their arms outward and wakes themselves up. Secure swaddling or weighted solutions are essential to safely dampening this reflex.
*   **Melatonin Production:** Newborns do not produce their own melatonin (the sleep hormone) until roughly 8 to 12 weeks of age. Creating a highly consistent, dark, and sensory-soothing sleep environment is critical to establishing their natural circadian rhythm.
