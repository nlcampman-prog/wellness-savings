## Rebuilding the Core: Why You Must Start from the Floor Up

When most women think about "getting their core back," they immediately picture planks, sit-ups, and crunches. But this is a massive physiological mistake.

Your core is not just your visible six-pack abs. It is a highly integrated, biological pressure system:
*   **The Roof:** Your diaphragm (breathing muscle).
*   **The Walls:** Your transverse abdominis (the deep, corset-like core muscles).
*   **The Floor:** Your **pelvic floor muscles** (the hammock of muscles supporting your bladder, bowel, and uterus).

During pregnancy and birth, your pelvic floor and deep abdominal walls undergo extreme physical stretching and pressure. Doing traditional ab exercises like crunches actually increases intra-abdominal pressure, pushing *down* on a weakened pelvic floor and pushing *out* on separating abdominal walls. This can worsen leaking and perpetuate the "postpartum pooch."

To return to fitness safely, **you must rebuild your pelvic floor and deep core first—before adding external physical resistance.**
