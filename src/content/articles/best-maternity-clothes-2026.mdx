## What to Look For in High-Quality Maternity Clothes

Before investing in maternity wear, look for these three core fabric and design elements:

1.  **4-Way Stretch & Shape Retention:** Look for fabrics containing high percentages of Lycra, Spandex, or Elastane. These materials stretch effortlessly to accommodate your growing bump and—crucially—snap back to their original shape without sagging.
2.  **Hypoallergenic & Breathable Fibers:** Pregnancy hormones naturally raise your body temperature, making hot flashes and skin sensitivities highly common. Prioritize breathable, moisture-wicking natural fibers like organic cotton, bamboo, and premium modal.
3.  **Postpartum and Nursing Transition:** To maximize your return on investment, look for garments that feature discrete feeding panels, wrap designs, or clip-down snaps that transition seamlessly into everyday **nursing wear** postpartum.
