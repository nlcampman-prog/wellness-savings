While a 75-ingredient list sounds incredibly impressive, it presents a unique scientific double-edged sword:

### The Cons: The "Proprietary Blend" Dilemma
*   **Proprietary Blends:** To protect their recipe, AG1 groups many of their ingredients into proprietary complexes. While we know the total weight of the complex, we cannot see the exact milligram dosage of individual ingredients like Ashwagandha or CoQ10. This means some of the premium herbs might be present in lower, non-clinical doses.
*   **Not a Replacement for Real Food:** A powder can never replace the physical fiber, hydration, and nutritional matrix of eating whole organic fruits and vegetables.
