## WiFi vs. Non-WiFi Baby Monitors: The Core Choice

Before looking at specific brands, it is crucial to understand the functional difference between these two technologies:

### 1. WiFi / Smart Monitors (Best for Remote Viewing & AI Tracking)
These monitors connect directly to your home's wireless network and stream the camera feed to a dedicated app on your smartphone or tablet. 

*   **Pros:** Unlimited range—you can check in on your baby from the office, the grocery store, or even while traveling. They often feature advanced AI tracking (breathing movement, sleep coaching, automatic cry notifications).
*   **Cons:** Rely entirely on your internet connection. If your WiFi goes down, you lose the feed. There is also a small, inherent risk of digital hacking if strong passwords are not used.

### 2. Non-WiFi / Local-Link Monitors (Best for Security & Instant Reliability)
These monitors use a closed-loop **FHSS (Frequency-Hopping Spread Spectrum)** radio signal to transmit the video feed directly from the camera to a dedicated handheld parent screen.

*   **Pros:** 100% hack-proof and secure since they do not connect to the internet. They work instantly out of the box and are completely independent of internet outages.
*   **Cons:** Limited range (typically up to 1,000 feet within the home). You cannot view your baby when you are away from the house.
