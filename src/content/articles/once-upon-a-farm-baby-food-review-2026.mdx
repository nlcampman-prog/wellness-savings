## The Cold-Pressed Difference: What is HPP?

The secret to Once Upon A Farm’s exceptional flavor and nutritional preservation is **High-Pressure Processing (HPP)**—commonly known as cold-pressing. 

Instead of using high heat to sterilize the food, Once Upon A Farm blends organic ingredients cold, seals them into their pouches, and subjects the pouches to extreme water pressure. 

This high pressure naturally inactivates any harmful bacteria, yeast, or mold, while completely preserving:
1.  **Vitamins and Antioxidants:** Delicate nutrients like Vitamin C and B vitamins remain biologically active and undamaged.
2.  **Natural Color & Texture:** The purées retain their vibrant, appetizing natural colors and thick, fresh-cooked textures.
3.  **Real, Fresh Flavor:** Because the natural sugars and volatile aromatic compounds in organic fruits and vegetables aren’t carmelized or cooked off by heat, the food actually tastes like real, delicious produce.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Why do Once Upon A Farm pouches need to be refrigerated?
Because Once Upon A Farm utilizes HPP (cold-pressing) instead of high-heat pasteurization, their food is fresh and raw. This means it must be stored in the refrigerator to prevent fermentation and spoil, just like fresh juice or raw produce.

### Can I take Once Upon A Farm pouches on the go?
Yes! While they must be stored in the fridge, Once Upon A Farm pouches can safely sit at room temperature (in a diaper bag, backpack, or lunchbox) for **up to 4 hours**. For longer outings, simply pop them into an insulated lunch bag with a standard ice pack.

### Are the pouches recyclable?
Once Upon A Farm is committed to eco-friendly packaging. They have partnered with **TerraCycle** to provide a free, easy recycling program for their plastic pouches, ensuring that empty packaging is kept out of landfills and repurposed.
