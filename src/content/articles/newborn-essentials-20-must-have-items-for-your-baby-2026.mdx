## 1. Safe Sleep Essentials (Items 1 - 4)
Creating a safe sleep environment is the single most important task for a newborn nursery.
1.  **Crib, Bassinet, or Pack 'n Play:** A safe, sturdy, and JPMA-certified sleeping space.
2.  **Breathable Mattress:** A firm, non-toxic, and breathable mattress to reduce suffocation risks.
3.  **Swaddles & Sleep Sacks (3-4):** Swaddling is the ultimate way to soothe a newborn and prevent their startle reflex from waking them up.
4.  **White Noise Machine:** Mimics the loud, whooshing sounds of the womb, helping your baby transition into deep sleep.

## 4. Clothing & Layette Essentials (Items 13 - 16)
Keep baby clothes simple, comfortable, and easy to change.
13. **Two-Way Zipper Onesies (5-7):** Avoid clothes with snaps! Two-way zippers are the ultimate design because they allow you to perform quick middle-of-the-night diaper changes without completely exposing your baby's chest to the cold.
14. **Organic Cotton Bodysuits (5-7):** Soft, breathable, and gentle on sensitive newborn skin.
15. **Baby Mittens & Booties:** Prevents your newborn from scratching their face with their sharp little fingernails and keeps tiny toes warm.
16. **Gentle, Hypoallergenic Laundry Detergent:** Formulated without harsh chemicals or artificial fragrances to protect your baby's delicate skin barrier.

## 5. Hygiene & Diapering Essentials (Items 17 - 20)
Diapering and bathing a newborn requires gentle, chemical-free care.
17. **Eco-Friendly Diapers & Wipes:** Choose chlorine-free, fragrance-free, and hypoallergenic options to prevent painful diaper rash.
18. **Gentle Baby Wash & Lotion:** Organic, plant-based, tear-free formulas.
19. **Infant Bath Tub:** Features a supportive, non-slip sling to hold your slippery newborn safely in place.
20. **Newborn Grooming & Health Kit:** Includes a digital thermometer, a nasal aspirator (snot sucker), and safe, rounded baby nail clippers or an electric nail file.

## Seasoned Mom Budget Hacks for 2026

*   **Don't Buy Newborn Clothes in Bulk:** Many newborns are born too large for "Newborn" size clothes, or outgrow them within two weeks. Focus your budget primarily on 0-3 month and 3-6 month sizes instead.
*   **Skip the Expensive Crib Bedding Sets:** For safe sleep, a crib should be completely empty except for a firm mattress and a tight, fitted sheet. Pillows, bumpers, and heavy blankets are dangerous safety hazards and a waste of money.
