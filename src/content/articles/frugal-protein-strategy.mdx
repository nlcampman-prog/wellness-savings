## FAQ: The Frugal Protein Strategy

### Is AG1 a protein supplement?

No. AG1 is a greens / foundational nutrition powder with about **2 grams of protein** per serving at **$2.63–$3.30**. Use it as greens if you love it. Do not use it as breakfast protein. The five picks above all beat it on protein by a wide margin.

### Can I really get 65g+ of protein for about $5?

Yes, if you stack cheap grams instead of buying one expensive scoop. Flourish breakfast (23–24g) + collagen in coffee (11g) + Rival whey (25g) lands around **59–60g**. A second collagen scoop pushes you over **70g** for about **$4–$5** after the codes in this article. That is **cost-effective nutrition**, not a challenge-account gimmick.

### Is collagen enough protein on its own?

No. Hydrolyzed collagen is **97% protein** and a bargain at 11g, but it is not a complete amino acid profile for muscle. I treat it as a booster on top of Flourish, Equip, Rival, or Level-1 — not the only protein I eat.

### I get bloated from whey. What should I buy first?

Start with **Equip Prime Protein** (grass-fed beef isolate, dairy-free, 21g) and **Flourish** if you tolerate the whey pancake line — or use Flourish's plant-based mix. Skip Rival Clean Whey until you know dairy sits well. I walked through the gut piece in [what's really in your protein powder](/articles/whats-really-in-your-protein-powder-hidden-ingredients-2026).

### Do any of these codes stack?

**Perfect Supplements** is the stacking champion: **UNMOM10** plus their automatic bulk discount when you buy 3+. Most other brands are one code or one affiliate link. Rival and 1st Phorm are link-based. Do not stack random influencer codes on top and hope — checkout will just reject the second one.

### Is this safe while breastfeeding or pregnant?

Food-first protein (Flourish pancakes, a clean scoop, collagen) is the category I am comfortable talking about as *food*. Prenatal-specific nutrition is **Needed**, not a random pre-workout. I am not your doctor. If you are pregnant or nursing, run the actual product and your situation past your provider. Then use the codes so you are not overpaying.

### What about plant-based protein?

Flourish has a plant-based pancake mix. For powders, I still prefer beef isolate or whey for completeness and digestibility, which I explain in the [clean protein powders guide](/articles/clean-protein-powders-women-no-artificial-junk-2026). If you are fully plant-based, look for a pea + rice blend and still do the protein-per-dollar math against that $3 greens scoop.

### How much protein do I actually need?

More than the old RDA, for most active women and moms. I break down RDA vs. optimal in [Protein 101](/articles/protein-101-how-much-do-women-actually-need). The short version: aiming for **~30g at meals** is how 100g days happen without living in a shaker bottle.
