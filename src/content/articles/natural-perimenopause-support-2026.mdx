## What is Perimenopause? Understanding the Hormonal Shift

Many women mistakenly believe that menopause happens overnight. In reality, **menopause is a single point in time**—defined as the exact mark when a woman has gone 12 consecutive months without a menstrual period.

**Perimenopause**, on the other hand, is the entire transitional journey leading up to that point. It can last anywhere from **4 to 10 years**. 

During this phase, your hormone levels do not just steadily decline; rather, they fluctuate wildly. You can experience periods of "estrogen dominance" followed by rapid estrogen crashes, alongside a steady, continuous drop in progesterone (your calming, mood-stabilizing hormone).

These fluctuations are what trigger the classic symptoms of perimenopause:

1.  **Vascular Fluctuations:** Hot flashes, night sweats, and sudden heart palpitations.
2.  **Neurological Fatigue:** Severe brain fog, sleep disturbances, afternoon fatigue, and heightened anxiety.
3.  **Metabolic Sluggishness:** Unexplained weight gain, particularly around the lower abdomen, alongside muscle loss.
4.  **Tissue Changes:** Thinning hair, dry skin, and joint stiffness.

To successfully navigate perimenopause naturally, your supplemental routine should address three critical physiological bottlenecks:

*   **Adaptogenic Adrenal Support:** Your adrenal glands take over sex hormone production as your ovaries retire. Supporting your adrenals prevents severe hormonal crashes.
