## Is Stork Exchange Safe? The Inspection & Cleanliness Question

This is the question every parent asks — and it's the right one. Buying used baby gear isn't the same as buying a used coffee table. Safety standards for car seats, sleep products, and strollers are non-negotiable.

### Car Seat Safety
**Stork Exchange's policy:** Car seats can be listed and sold on the platform, but listings must include the expiration date (all car seats expire, typically 6–10 years from manufacture) and confirmation that the seat has never been in an accident. The platform's messaging strongly emphasizes that buyers should verify these details before purchasing.

**My take as a parent:** I'm comfortable buying a used infant car seat that's less than 3 years old and has documented accident-free history. Infant seats are used for such a short window that they're often in near-new condition. For convertible seats (used longer, more wear), I'd buy new or open-box only. But every parent's risk tolerance is different — and that's okay.

### Stroller & Gear Safety
For non-car-seat items, the safety equation is simpler. Look for:
- **Working brakes and harnesses** — test them immediately upon arrival
- **No structural damage** — check frames, joints, and folding mechanisms
- **Complete parts** — confirm all hardware, wheels, and accessories are included
- **No recalls** — check the CPSC website for any open recalls on the model

### Cleanliness
Every Stork Exchange item I've received has been clean, but my practice is to do a thorough wipe-down on arrival regardless. Fabric items get laundered; hard surfaces get disinfected. This is standard practice for any used purchase and shouldn't deter you — it takes 20 minutes.

## Selling on Stork Exchange: The Other Side

I've sold on Stork Exchange too, and the experience was straightforward. The platform takes a commission (typically 10–15%), which is comparable to other specialized marketplaces.

**What sells best:**
- Premium strollers (Nuna, UPPAbaby) — strong demand, good resale value
- Infant car seats — high turnover since they're outgrown quickly
- High-end high chairs (Stokke Tripp Trapp) — coveted at any price
- Baby carriers and wraps — small, easy to ship

**Tips for selling successfully:**
- **Take clear, well-lit photos** from multiple angles. Show any wear honestly — it builds trust and prevents disputes.
- **List seasonally.** Strollers sell better in spring. Holiday-themed items sell better in November.
- **Price competitively.** Check what similar items are listed for and price 5–10% below the average if you want a quick sale.
- **Keep original packaging if possible.** It increases perceived value significantly.
