## EAAs are not a loophole for skipping dinner

Protein food still wins. Grams still count. [How much protein women actually need](/articles/protein-101-how-much-do-women-actually-need/) did not change because tablets exist.

Use Perfect Aminos when:

- you **cannot stomach a shake** but you already lifted,
- you are **hours from a real meal** and the alternative is a handful of crackers,
- you did an **early workout** and breakfast is after school drop-off,
- whey **bloats you** and you are not in the mood to debug dairy today.

Do **not** use them when:

- you have not eaten since yesterday and you are calling tablets "lunch,"
- you are trying to lose weight by deleting meals,
- you think 99% utilization means you can ignore chicken.

If you want food-first protein that is not a shaker at all, that is [Flourish pancakes](/articles/flourish-protein-pancakes-review-2026/) and the [frugal protein list](/articles/frugal-protein-strategy/). EAAs sit *beside* that, not on top of it as a personality.

## FAQ

### Can EAAs replace whey forever?
They can replace the *shake* on specific days. They should not replace *protein intake*. Whey is food-ish. EAAs are the bricks without the wall.

### Will I break a fast?
If you are fasting for a protocol your clinician set, ask them. If you are "fasting" because you skipped breakfast, eat breakfast.

### Tablets or powder?
Tablets travel. Powder mixes. I use tablets because I will not mix a third drink while packing lunches.

### Is this for kids?
No. This article is for adults who lift and forget to eat. Kids need food.

## The rule I keep

If I can eat, I eat. If I cannot eat and I already trained, **Perfect Aminos**. If I want a shake that feels like a meal, **Momentum**. If it is morning and I have a griddle, **Flourish**.

EAAs are a tool for the gap. They are a bad religion.
