## Nice-to-Haves (Convenient, But Not Essential)

These items are highly convenient and make life significantly easier, but you can get by without them if you are on a strict budget or have limited space:

1.  **High-Tech Baby Monitors:** Video monitors that track heart rate or oxygen are nice for peace of mind, but a standard audio or basic video monitor is completely sufficient.
2.  **Bottle Sterilizers:** Boiling bottles in hot water works just as well as an expensive countertop sterilizer.
3.  **Wipe Warmers:** Most babies do not mind room-temperature wipes, and warmers can sometimes harbor bacteria if not cleaned frequently.
4.  **Premium Baby Clothes:** Newborns grow incredibly fast and spit up frequently. Focus your registry on budget-friendly, organic cotton onesies (with two-way zippers!) rather than expensive designer outfits.

## Tips for Creating a Stress-Free Registry in 2026

*   **Registry-Hop for Welcome Boxes:** Many major registry platforms offer free welcome boxes packed with high-quality baby samples (bottles, pacifiers, lotions) and provide a **15% completion discount** on remaining items. Register at 2-3 platforms to maximize these benefits!
*   **Ask for Gift Cards:** Add a fund for maternal postpartum recovery support, diaper funds, or organic meal delivery subscriptions (like Little Spoon) to help save you time during the early postpartum weeks.
*   **Focus on Longevity:** Prioritize items that grow with your baby. For example, the **Maxi-Cosi Emme 360** accommodates infants starting at 5 lbs all the way up to 100 lbs, saving you from buying multiple seats later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I start my baby registry?
Most parents start their baby registry around the **12th to 16th week of pregnancy**, right around the end of the first trimester. This gives you plenty of time to research products, read reviews, and share the registry link before your baby shower (which typically occurs around week 30 to 34).

### How many swaddles do I need?
We recommend putting **3 to 4 high-quality swaddles** on your registry. Babies frequently spit up or blow out of diapers, and having a few backups ensures you aren't running emergency laundry cycles at 2:00 AM.
