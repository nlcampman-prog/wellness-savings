## The Recovery Kit Philosophy: Nourish, Soothe, and Support

When building your postpartum recovery kit, it is important to treat your body with the same gentleness and reverence you show your newborn. A high-quality kit should target three core areas of recovery:

1.  **Systemic Nutritional Support:** Restoring lost vitamins, balancing hormones, and fueling tissue repair from the inside out.
2.  **Topical, Clean Physical Care:** Using organic, non-toxic botanicals to soothe tender tissues and promote perineal or incision healing.
3.  **High-Comfort Loungewear:** Wearing soft, breathable fabrics that support nursing access, don't rub against incisions, and make you feel human.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When should I prepare my postpartum recovery kit?
I highly recommend having your postpartum recovery essentials fully assembled and stocked in your bathroom by your **34th to 36th week of pregnancy**. You do not want to be sending your partner on a midnight run for witch hazel pads or nursing balm on your first night home from the hospital!

### How long does postpartum physical recovery take?
While the acute healing phase typically takes about 6 to 8 weeks, full internal and tissue restoration can take up to a year. Be incredibly patient with your body, continue your postnatal nutrition, and prioritize rest.
