## What is NAD+? The Fuel Inside Your Cellular Engines

To understand why NAD+ supplements are so vital, we have to look inside your cells. **NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide)** is a critical co-enzyme found in every single living cell in your body. It acts as the primary spark plug for your **mitochondria**—the tiny power plants inside your cells that produce ATP (the raw chemical energy your body uses to think, move, and heal).

Without sufficient NAD+, your cellular engines stall.

The biological catch is that **our natural NAD+ levels decline dramatically as we age**. By the time we reach 40, our NAD+ levels have dropped by roughly **50%**. By age 60, they have plummeted to less than **15%** of our youthful levels. This decline is a primary driver of:

*   Chronic mid-afternoon fatigue and sluggishness
*   Accelerated cognitive "brain fog" and memory slips
*   Slower physical recovery from workouts and daily stress
*   Systemic, low-grade cellular inflammation (often called "inflammaging")

## The Alphabet Soup: NR vs. NMN vs. NAD+

Can you just swallow raw NAD+? Technically yes, but it is highly unstable and poorly absorbed by the digestive tract. To effectively boost your cellular levels, science relies on **precursors**—smaller, highly stable molecules that your body easily absorbs and converts into active NAD+ inside your cells.

There are two primary precursors dominating the market in 2026:

1.  **NR (Nicotinamide Riboside):** A highly stable, thoroughly researched vitamin B3 derivative. NR has been proven in dozens of human clinical trials to safely and effectively elevate NAD+ levels by up to 60% with exceptional oral bioavailability.
2.  **NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide):** A slightly larger molecule that is a direct step before NAD+ in the cellular synthesis pathway. While highly popular, NMN faces unique regulatory discussions in some regions, though it remains a powerful cellular focus.
