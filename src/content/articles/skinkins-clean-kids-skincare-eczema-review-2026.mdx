## What Is Skinkins?

Skinkins is a kids' skincare brand launched with a specific mission: to create products that support and protect children's developing skin barrier without the harsh preservatives, synthetic fragrances, and endocrine-disrupting chemicals found in many mainstream baby and kids' products.

**The brand's core philosophy rests on three pillars:**

1. **Barrier-first formulation.** Children's skin has a thinner stratum corneum (the outermost protective layer) than adult skin, which means it loses moisture faster and absorbs irritants more easily. Skinkins formulates every product with this biological reality in mind.

2. **Strict ingredient standards.** No parabens, phthalates, sulfates, synthetic fragrances, mineral oil, or formaldehyde-releasing preservatives. This isn't marketing fluff — it's a genuine clean formulation standard that puts Skinkins in the same league as brands like Earth Mama Organics (which I've [reviewed here](/earth-mama-organics-review-2026)).

3. **Kid-friendly experience.** Foaming pumps, gentle scents (from natural sources only), and packaging kids actually want to use — because the best skincare routine is the one that actually happens.

## In-Depth: Skinkins Soothing Face & Body Lotion

This is Skinkins' hero moisturizer, and the formula is built around three ingredients that dermatologists consistently recommend for compromised skin barriers:

- **Ceramides:** These are lipids naturally found in healthy skin that form the "mortar" between skin cells. When the barrier is damaged (eczema, dryness, irritation), ceramide levels drop. Topical ceramides help rebuild that barrier.
- **Shea butter:** An occlusive that seals moisture in without being greasy. Skinkins uses unrefined shea, which retains more of its natural vitamin content.
- **Calendula:** A botanical with centuries of clinical use for wound healing and skin inflammation.

The lotion is medium-weight — heavier than a standard drugstore lotion but lighter than a balm. It absorbs within about 30 seconds and doesn't leave a film, which means my kids don't complain about feeling "sticky." For my eczema-prone twin, I layer this under a heavier occlusive at night; for my other kids, it works as a standalone all-over moisturizer.

## In-Depth: Skinkins Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50

Sunscreen is the product category where I'm most particular — and most frustrated by the market. Many "baby" sunscreens are thick, ghostly pastes that turn your child into a chalk outline. Others use chemical UV filters (oxybenzone, octinoxate) that some parents — myself included — prefer to avoid on young children.

Skinkins uses **22.5% non-nano zinc oxide**, which means:
- **Broad-spectrum mineral protection** without chemical absorbers
- **Non-nano particles** won't absorb into the bloodstream (the primary concern with nano zinc)
- **22.5% concentration** provides real SPF 50 protection, not the minimum zinc levels some "mineral" sunscreens use to claim the label

**The application experience** is better than most mineral sunscreens I've tried, but it's not invisible. There's a slight white cast that fades as you rub it in, and it takes about 90 seconds of rubbing to fully blend on lighter skin tones. On darker skin, the cast may be more noticeable — a common issue with all zinc-based sunscreens.

One of the things I appreciate about Skinkins is ingredient transparency. Here's a closer look:

### The Good (Star Ingredients)
| Ingredient | Function | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Colloidal oatmeal** | Anti-inflammatory, moisture barrier | FDA-approved for eczema; reduces itching within hours |
| **Ceramides (NP, AP, EOP)** | Barrier repair | Ceramides make up 50% of skin's natural barrier |
| **Calendula officinalis** | Anti-inflammatory, wound healing | Clinically studied for diaper dermatitis and eczema |
| **Chamomile (bisabolol)** | Soothing, anti-redness | The bisabolol component specifically reduces UV-induced redness |
| **Glycerin** | Humectant | Draws water into the skin; one of the most effective and well-tolerated moisturizing ingredients |

### What's Conspicuously Absent
- **No synthetic fragrance:** Scent comes from the chamomile and calendula extracts themselves, not added perfume.
- **No parabens or phthalates:** Endocrine-disrupting preservatives that are still legal in US cosmetics but banned in the EU.
- **No formaldehyde-releasing preservatives:** This includes DMDM hydantoin, quaternium-15, and others commonly used in "tear-free" formulations.
- **No sulfates (SLS/SLES):** The foaming action comes from decyl glucoside, a gentle glucose-based surfactant.
