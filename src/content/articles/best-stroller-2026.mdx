## What to Look For in a Premium Stroller

Before making your investment, ensure your stroller meets these baseline criteria:

1.  **Safety Certification:** Look for strollers that are certified by the JPMA (Juvenile Products Manufacturers Association) and feature a secure, easy-to-adjust 5-point safety harness.
2.  **The One-Hand Fold:** For busy parents holding a baby in one arm, a stroller that can be folded quickly and compactly with a single hand is an absolute lifesaver.
3.  **Terrain & Suspension:** If you walk on cracked pavements, gravel paths, or grass, prioritize strollers with all-wheel suspension and large, puncture-proof rubber wheels to ensure a smooth, comfortable ride for your baby.
4.  **Car Seat Compatibility (Travel Systems):** If you have a newborn, ensure the stroller can seamlessly connect to your infant car seat (like the premium Maxi-Cosi infant carriers) without requiring complex, separate adapters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a standard stroller and a jogging stroller?
*   **Standard Strollers:** Have smaller, solid plastic or rubber wheels and are designed for smooth surfaces like malls, sidewalks, and paved pathways. They are highly maneuverable but cannot handle rough terrain or fast speeds.
*   **Jogging Strollers:** Feature large, air-filled pneumatic tires (typically 12 to 16 inches) and advanced rear suspension. The front wheel can be locked straight to prevent wobbling at high speeds, providing a safe, shock-absorbing ride for your baby while you run.

### At what age can a baby sit in a standard stroller?
A newborn baby does not have the head and neck control required to sit in a standard stroller seat. Therefore, from **birth to roughly 6 months**, you must use a stroller that can lay completely flat, connect to a compatible bassinet, or attach to your infant car seat (creating a unified travel system). Once your baby can sit up independently (typically around 6 months), they can safely transition to the standard stroller seat.
