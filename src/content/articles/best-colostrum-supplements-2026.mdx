## What is Bovine Colostrum? Understanding the Biology of Cellular Repair

Before we compare the brands, let's talk about what colostrum actually is. Colostrum is the thick, nutrient-dense pre-milk fluid produced by female mammals in the first few days immediately following childbirth—before standard breast milk comes in.

It is an evolutionary masterpiece. Colostrum is not a food designed to provide bulk calories; rather, it is a highly concentrated **biological instruction manual** packed with bio-active compounds designed to protect, seal, and repair cellular barriers.

For adults and children, high-quality grass-fed bovine colostrum delivers three primary therapeutic categories:

1.  **Immunoglobulins (IgG, IgA, IgM):** These are clinical-grade antibodies that physically neutralise harmful bacteria, environmental viruses, and gut pathogens, acting as an immediate shield for your immune system.
2.  **Natural Growth Factors (IgF-1, IgF-2, TGF-Beta):** Powerful peptide hormones that trigger protein synthesis, promote satellite cell proliferation to repair sore muscles, and support natural collagen synthesis for radiant skin and hair.
3.  **Lactoferrin & Lactalbumins:** Highly active antimicrobial proteins that starve harmful bacteria of iron while feeding and protecting beneficial gut probiotics, helping to heal a leaky gut.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Does colostrum contain dairy/lactose?
Yes, colostrum is a dairy-derived product and naturally contains small amounts of lactose and casein. However, because it is a pre-milk fluid, the concentration of lactose is much lower than in regular milk. Many people with mild lactose intolerance digest cold-processed colostrum like **Cowboy Colostrum** or **Ancestral** beautifully. If you have a severe, diagnosed dairy allergy, you should avoid bovine colostrum.
