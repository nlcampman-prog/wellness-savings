## The Root Causes of "Mom Fatigue" (Why Coffee Isn't Fixing It)

To solve the fatigue problem, we have to understand *why* you are tired in the first place. For most moms, exhaustion is not just a lack of sleep; it is a combination of three physiological bottlenecks:

1.  **Mitochondrial Dysfunction:** Your mitochondria are the tiny engines inside your cells that produce ATP (cellular energy). If they lack the necessary co-factors (like CoQ10, B vitamins, and iron), your body cannot produce energy efficiently, no matter how much caffeine you drink.
2.  **Adrenal Exhaustion (Cortisol Spikes):** Chronic stress causes your adrenal glands to continuously pump out cortisol. Over time, this leads to adrenal fatigue, disrupting your natural circadian rhythm and leaving you sluggish all day.
3.  **Nutrient Depletion:** Pregnancy, nursing, and stress heavily deplete a woman's body of essential trace minerals, bioavailable B vitamins, and heme iron. This depletion is a primary driver of brain fog and fatigue.
