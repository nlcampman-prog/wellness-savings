## The Core Advantages of Toddler Sleep Sacks

If you are on the fence about keeping your toddler in a sleep sack instead of introducing a loose blanket, let's look at why wearable blankets are superior:

1.  **Prevents Crib Climbing:** Once a toddler figures out how to hoist their foot onto the top rail of their crib, a dangerous fall is highly likely. A sleep sack gathers their legs together, making it physically impossible to lift their foot high enough to climb over the rail, extending their crib lifespan safely.
2.  **No Loose Bedding Risks:** Wearable blankets eliminate any risks of suffocation or entanglement associated with loose quilts or heavy blankets.
3.  **Prevents Late-Night Awakenings:** Because the blanket is worn, it cannot be kicked off. Your child stays warm, cozy, and asleep all night long.
4.  **The Ultimate Sleep Association:** Putting on a sleep sack is a powerful, visual, and physical cue that signals to your child's brain: *"The routine is done, and it is time to sleep."*

## Understanding TOG Ratings: Finding the Right Thermal Weight

When shopping for a sleep sack, you will notice a number labeled **TOG (Thermal Overall Grade)**. TOG is a measure of thermal resistance—telling you how effectively a fabric retains body heat. 

Choosing the wrong TOG can lead to your toddler overheating (a dangerous sleep risk) or shivering. Use this helpful guide to choose the right TOG for your home’s temperature:

*   **0.5 TOG (Ultra-Lightweight):** Best for hot summer months or warm nurseries (**74°F to 78°F**). Usually made of single-layer bamboo or thin muslin.
*   **1.0 TOG (Year-Round):** The most versatile, year-round weight for standard rooms (**68°F to 73°F**). Usually made of double-layer cotton.
*   **2.5 TOG (Winter Weight):** Designed for freezing winter nights or cold nurseries (**61°F to 67°F**). Usually quilted or lightly padded.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When should a toddler stop using a sleep sack?
Generally, kids can stay in a standard toddler sleep sack until they reach **35 inches** in height or are ready to transition out of their crib and into a toddler bed. Once they are walking and climbing out of bed independently, you can transition them to a "walker sleep sack" (which features foot holes for mobility) or a standard toddler pillow and blanket set.

### Are weighted sleep sacks safe for active toddlers?
Yes! High-quality weighted sacks like the **Dreamland Baby** are designed with gentle, evenly distributed weight that meets or exceeds all physical safety standards. They allow your child to roll over, stand, and move freely inside their crib, ensuring their movement is never restricted.
