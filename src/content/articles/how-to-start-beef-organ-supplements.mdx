## What you are swallowing (so the smell makes sense)

Liver is high in **vitamin A, B12, copper, and heme iron**. Heart is a muscle food (CoQ10 shows up in the marketing). Kidney is the one people skip because of the idea of kidney.

Freeze-dried means the water is gone and the nutrients are concentrated. Capsules keep you from tasting it *if you do not breathe in when the lid comes off*. That is the whole skill.

You are not "detoxing." You are eating an organ in slow motion.

## Week 1: half the serving, with food, not on an empty stomach

The #1 way people quit: six large capsules on an empty stomach, then a wave of "why is my life like this."

**What I actually do:**

1. Take **half the label serving** for seven days. If the bottle says 6, start at 3.
2. **With breakfast.** Fat in the meal helps. Coffee-only is how nausea stories start.
3. Do not open the bottle under your nose like a wine tasting.
4. Swallow with a full glass of water. Dry-swallowing six capsules is a hobby for other people.

If you feel nothing at day seven, that is normal. These are food. Food is not a pre-workout.

If you feel flu-ish or your skin goes weird, stop. Vitamin A is not a more-is-more vitamin. That is the opposite of the TikTok "just take more."

## The taste problem is usually the lid

- Store them somewhere cool and dry. A hot car turns the smell up.
- Do not dump capsules into your palm and pause to text.
- If even capsules are a no, you are allowed to skip this trend. Steak and a prenatal (if you still need one) is a valid life.

## Who should not "just try it"

- Anyone told to **limit vitamin A or iron**.
- **Pregnancy** unless your clinician is in on it — liver is high in vitamin A.
- People who use organs as a substitute for eating because the bottle feels like atonement.

If your goal is energy and your sleep is four hours, fix sleep before you buy spleen.

## FAQ

### How long until I "feel" it?
Some people notice energy in a couple of weeks. Some people notice nothing except that they swallowed capsules. If your ferritin was the issue, labs beat vibes. Give it 30 days at a full serving only if week one was fine.

### Can I take them with a prenatal?
Maybe. Prenatals already have vitamin A and iron. Stacking liver on top is how you accidentally mega-dose. Ask the person who knows your prenatal. [Needed](/articles/needed-prenatal-supplement-review-2026/) is the prenatal I actually send people to — do not freelance the stack.

### Why do people say it balances hormones?
Organs are nutrient-dense. Nutrients help a body that was running on toast. That is not the same as a hormone clinic. Be suspicious of anyone who says a capsule replaced their endocrinologist.

### Can kids take them?
Not from my blog. Pediatric dosing is a clinician conversation. I am a former pediatric SLP, not your child's doctor.
